LEMON MASTER ASSISTANT - local working version

On the master bus, underneath the headphone controls:
ON / OFF enables or bypasses mastering for playback and normal song exports.
OPEN opens the controls and reuses your existing settings.
Opening it for the first time leaves mastering OFF until you enable it.

Enable it, open the controls, play a representative section of your song and
run Master Assist. Review and apply its suggestion, then adjust the controls
to taste. The assistant listens for about a minute. Reference matching is
also available. Save your Lemon project to keep your settings.

Export the song normally. Keep Master FX enabled in export settings.
The global mixer FX bypass also bypasses the assistant. Enabling the
assistant restores the master effects group, including its other active slots.
Headphone correction remains monitor-only.

Powered by the oXygen engine by Wamphyre, adapted for Lemon Studio.
This is a local Windows working update, not a public release.

The main view has Bass, Body, Air, Width and Loudness knobs. Drag the three
tone handles on the EQ curve, or adjust their knobs. Detailed controls opens
the full rack. The curve shows the actual combined EQ response.
